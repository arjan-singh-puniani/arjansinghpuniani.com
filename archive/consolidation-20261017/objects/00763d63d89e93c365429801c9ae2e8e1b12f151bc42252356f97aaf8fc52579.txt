# Rally House v17.1 — Toy Contact

This is a **surgical follow-up** to the Rally House v17 tactile-build patch.

It targets the exact problem visible in the close-up playtest: the ball is readable, but the racket still reads like a ring and the hit does not have a convincing impact frame.

## What changes

### Racket
- Removes the second torus that visually read as another hollow ring.
- Keeps one stronger racket frame.
- Adds a faint translucent string-bed surface.
- Adds five vertical and five horizontal procedural strings.
- Strings rotate with the real racket frame.

### Contact
- Uses the live `racketContactFrame()` rather than a vague time fallback.
- The staged ball converges onto the live string-bed center.
- Adds a **36 ms impact latch**, about two frames at 60 fps.
- During the latch the ball is flattened along the racket-normal axis.
- The ball launches only after the latch.

### Contact feedback
- Sound triggers at the same authoritative contact event.
- A small cream ring and tennis-green echo ring appear in the real racket plane.
- Four tiny pop dots expand away from the string bed.
- The burst decays in roughly 130 ms.
- The old impact ring no longer reappears at the racket when the ball later bounces.

### Swing
- Forehand and backhand follow-through are modestly amplified.
- Contact timing, contact target solving, and shot IK are not rewritten.

## Manual QA

Watch at least one serve, forehand, backhand, and volley.

Look for this sequence:

1. Ball visibly reaches the string bed.
2. Ball compresses on the strings for a tiny instant.
3. Cream/green pop occurs exactly there.
4. `THWACK` occurs at that same instant.
5. Ball leaves after the latch.
6. Green speed trail begins after launch.
7. No second contact flash appears when the ball bounces.

Also inspect the racket while idle/ready. It should read as **frame + strings**, not two nested rings.
