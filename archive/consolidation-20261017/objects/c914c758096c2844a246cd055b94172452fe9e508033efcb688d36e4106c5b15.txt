# Rally House RC3.4 — Cozy Atmosphere

This pass deepens club-mode atmosphere without changing Championship rules.

- Cooler, quieter ambient field with always-present warm practical-light pockets.
- Warm local specular response so wood, ceramic, glass and metal read as different materials.
- Subtle floor pools, fixture halos and string-light breathing rather than global bloom.
- Low procedural room-tone bed begins only after the browser has already unlocked audio through a player gesture.
- Tactile object interactions can prompt one or two nearby free club members to briefly glance/react, then automatically return their gaze.
- A CSS atmosphere layer adds a restrained vignette and warm light falloff in club mode and drops to 20% strength during Championship so ball/court readability stays intact.

The goal is a lived-in miniature sanctuary, not a copy of any reference artwork.
