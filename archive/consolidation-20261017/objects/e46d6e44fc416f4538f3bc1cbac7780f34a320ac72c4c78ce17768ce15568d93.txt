## Corrections made

- Right subscapularis: `skeleton` → `muscles`.
- Right suprascapular, subscapular, and circumflex scapular arteries: `skeleton` → `arteries`.
- Right suprascapular, subscapular, and circumflex scapular veins: `skeleton` → `veins`.

These corrections affect rendering materials, layer controls, isolation behavior, and educational presentation.

## Fidelity limit

The current 261 production OBJ meshes contain **no UV coordinates**. The cuff muscles therefore cannot support source-authored albedo, normal, roughness, or anatomically directional fiber textures without replacing/upgrading the assets. HoloAnatomy 1.0 keeps this limitation explicit rather than synthesizing false anatomy.

## New shoulder presentation

The candidate adds a source-faithful Rotator Cuff Studio with four curated views: Rotator cuff, Supraspinatus outlet, Anterior cuff, and Regional circulation. These views alter visibility/ghosting/camera composition only; they do not create missing structures.
