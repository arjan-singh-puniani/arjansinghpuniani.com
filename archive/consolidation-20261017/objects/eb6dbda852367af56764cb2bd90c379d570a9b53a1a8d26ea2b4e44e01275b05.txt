## Voice changes

The site now favors concrete statements over positioning language.

Examples:

- **Before:** “I build research tools, experimental interfaces, and neurotechnology prototypes.”  
  **After:** “I build research tools for neural engineering and neurotechnology.”

- **Before:** “Keep the measurements, assumptions, and limitations visible.”  
  **After:** “Show your work.”

- **Before:** “The interface is part of the measurement.”  
  **After:** “The interface can change the measurement.”

- **Before:** “A handoff is a data structure.”  
  **After:** “Good handoffs separate facts from interpretation.”

- **Before:** “Help research move into a product or workflow.”  
  **After:** “Work between research and product.”
