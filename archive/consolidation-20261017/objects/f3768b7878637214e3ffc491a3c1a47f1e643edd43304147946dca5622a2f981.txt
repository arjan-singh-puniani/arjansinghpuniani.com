# Rally House v17.4 — Cozy Life + Human Taylor + Steph

This patch targets the current v17.3+ architecture.

## What it implements

- Human Taylor visual direction; removes the old cat-ear geometry.
- Adds **Steph** as a real fifth NPC. The source stores only `Steph` / `steph`, never a surname.
- Gives Steph a full day schedule, dialogue, mind state, social weighting, cup possession, and one authored café scene.
- Makes old four-member CharacterMind saves backwards-compatible by inserting safe Steph defaults at load time.
- Turns `drink` into a readable raise → sip → lower cycle.
- Keeps the cup attached to the real hand.
- Adds a cup handle, visible liquid surface, and exactly three lightweight procedural steam wisps.
- Adds a focused `cozy-life.mjs` regression suite.

## Apply

Put this kit in your hardening/worktree folder or call the Python installer by full path.

```bash
cd "$HOME/Desktop/arjansinghpuniani-hardening"
python3 /path/to/apply_rally_house_v17_4_cozy_life.py
```

## Manual QA

1. Pick **Taylor**. Confirm she is fully human with long dark hair and no cat ears.
2. Find a scheduled drink moment (Contessa, Lucresia, Barbara, Leo, or Steph).
3. Confirm cup starts low, rises to the mouth, holds briefly, and lowers again.
4. Confirm steam originates from the cup and never exceeds a few wisps.
5. Let the day run and find **Steph**:
   - she should move through her schedule,
   - appear in the Club Book,
   - participate in ordinary crowd/navigation behavior,
   - be eligible for ambient social planning,
   - occasionally appear in her authored café scene.
6. Confirm existing rally spectating, racket contact and build mode still work.
7. Test the portfolio iframe after syncing `dist`.
