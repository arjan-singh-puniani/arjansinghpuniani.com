# HoloAnatomy Shoulder v0.5 — provenance

## Production anatomy source

- Dataset: BodyParts3D / ISA Release 4.0
- Organization: Database Center for Life Science (DBCLS)
- Source: https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/
- Current official license page: https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/lic.html
- Current official database license: CC BY 4.0
- Current official attribution: “BodyParts3D, © The Database Center for Life Science licensed under CC Attribution 4.0 International”

## v0.5 transformation policy

No anatomical vertices are altered by the v0.5 software patch.

The patch changes only:
- visibility/ghost state
- structure emphasis outlines
- camera presentation
- temporary dissection translation
- material rendering
- UI labels and learning interactions

The original registered mesh position remains the rebuild-practice ground truth.

## New geometry

None.

No tendon, labrum, capsule, bursa, ligament, nerve, vessel, pathology, footprint, or landmark geometry is generated or inferred.

## Presentation effects

The material shader adds a low-amplitude, non-directional surface-variation term and soft fill lighting. This is explicitly a **visualization effect**, not encoded histology or fiber direction.

## Known historical header discrepancy

Legacy BodyParts3D OBJ headers in the repository mention CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan. The current official DBCLS license page states CC BY 4.0 as of 2025-02-27. Preserve the embedded source headers and the current official license citation in provenance documentation.
