## Retained outside public pages

- RNEL team photographs and participant screenshots: require permission review.
- Zoom interview screenshots: contain names or potentially sensitive research context.
- Pitch videos: require a confidentiality, music-rights, and participant-consent review.
- Social and nightlife photographs: do not support the site’s professional purpose.
- Student identification, legal, medical, residential, and disciplinary material: excluded.
