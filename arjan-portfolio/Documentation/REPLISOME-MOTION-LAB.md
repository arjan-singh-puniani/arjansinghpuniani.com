# REPLISOME: a machine students can handle

Explore now runs continuously. In Orbit, visitors rotate and zoom around the moving fork. In Handle enzymes, they drag a molecular protein away from DNA to remove its activity, then return it to a highlighted docking target. A dropdown and Pull off DNA / Return to DNA buttons provide the same interaction with a keyboard or touch. Follow tracks the selected moving protein; Watch can follow successive handoffs. Prime, Clamp, Copy, Process and Seal replay baseline reaction cues and follow the relevant moving enzyme while retaining the selected perturbation. The global timeline and pause/slow playback remain available.

Pol δ and PCNA follow the visual fragment's 3′ end. Pol α approaches the starter, RFC approaches the primer–template junction, and FEN1 and ligase approach the completion junction. The growing end and strand labels are projected from their actual scene positions. The lagging template loop grows and releases in an illustrative trombone cycle. Pol ε removal leaves an exposed leading-template gap; restoration resumes copying at the modeled rate rather than instantly catching up. The lagging synthesis route runs away from the fork; this model does not invent walking legs or an atomic motor gait. Independently deposited structural assemblies retain their original coordinates in Structural evidence mode. Dragging returns to the illustrative surface representation.

## Perturbation model

Removing CMG stops new unwinding while exposed, already primed DNA can finish. Removal and restoration do not cause fork-coordinate jumps. Removing Pol α prevents new starters; RFC prevents fresh clamp loading; PCNA blocks modeled loading and Pol δ processivity. Pol δ removal stalls extension and its part of primer processing. FEN1 removal stalls the short-flap step. Ligase removal allows synthesis and clean nicks to accumulate, but blocks backbone sealing. RPA removal pauses modeled lagging copying. These are idealized stage-specific effects, not quantitative cellular inhibition assays: coupling, alternative primer processing, damage signaling and additional RPA responses are outside this model.

RNA removal/fill-in is a coarse fragment maturation state; the choreography illustrates the preceding primer's short-flap processing at the completion junction. Individual fragment lengths, timing, paths, contacts and ATP usage are educational. Independently solved protein structures are not a docked complete replisome or validated atomic trajectory. The optional reasoning studio remains supplementary.

Meaningful engine tests cover running exploration, isolated stalls, completion on exposed template, no jumps on restoration, accumulating nicks, primer and FEN1 blocks, deterministic replay and reset. Browser tests exercise actual 3D dragging and docking, camera lock during manipulation, moving follow targets, pause, phone controls, context restoration, fallback and full fragment gameplay. `tools/replisome/motion-qa.mjs` repeats embedded interactions in Chrome and WebKit at 1440×900, 1280×720 and 390×844, scans WCAG rules, checks live nicks and captures screenshots. `browser-qa.mjs` retains the portfolio regression checks. Physical iPhone thermal behavior and student learning outcomes are not measured.
