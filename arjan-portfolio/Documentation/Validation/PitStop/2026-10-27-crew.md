# Crew posture correction · 27 October 2026

The old crew used a 0.50 m pelvis height, wide permanently bent legs and a time-driven foot oscillation in every working pose. That produced the squat/waddle. Wheel-handler hand targets were also assigned across the torso on one side of the car, flaring the elbows.

`crew.js` now separates an upright 0.90 m pelvis posture from a stationary half-kneel at 0.51 m. Feet sit under the hips; thigh/shin lengths remain 0.43 m. A two-link bend solver keeps the knees in the working plane. Steps use modeled travel distance and alternating stance/swing phases; the upper body pivots at the pelvis. Service roles blend into the kneel at the wheel and stand for moving/clearing. Arms now use correctly sided grip targets and the same bend solver; standby hands rest down and the gun is carried above the waist. Jack operators and the supply-cart operator use the updated posture too. The manual challenge, simulation timestamps and release rules are unchanged.

Validated with 143 unit tests (including five new pose/limb/stance regressions), typecheck, targeted ESLint, and the complete 26-check garage/manual mouse + keyboard + replay/mobile/resource browser journey. Actual Chrome/Apple M2 screenshots and a quarter-speed motion clip were inspected through approach, service and clearance, without application errors. Production build output is retained with the evidence.
