## What changed and works

**Explore** opens directly on the car in a full-scale garage. Nine camera presets, orbit/zoom, component descriptions, bodywork hiding and running-gear isolation work. The old campus and engineering systems remain accessible through Race Engineer. The default view hides the analysis dock and inspector. Camera framing adapts to narrow viewports; the final side view includes the complete wing.

**Pit Stop Challenge** uses a timestamped reducer and the existing corner-snapshot contract. The visitor prepares the bay, positions equipment, raises the car, aligns/holds the wheel gun, removes and seats a wheel with axial drags, fastens it, clears equipment, lowers both jacks and requests release. Other corners must be retained and clear. Mouse/touch affordances have a complete keyboard alternative. Times, errors, local clean personal best and exact input replay work. This is procedural training, not pneumatic-force/torque calibration.

**Race Engineer** retains deterministic scenarios, unsafe-release holds, double stacking, exact seeking, mechanical inspection, timing, sensitivity and seeded uncertainty. The historical module joins real lap/stint/control/weather records, filters laps, trains ridge regression, reports a held-out comparison, evaluates nine actions and shows the source → model → futures → decision graph. The race replay is a separate nominal schematic illustration. Outputs are conditional chances of finishing first among the three entered cars, **not calibrated full-grid win forecasts**.

**Visual pass:** actual Blender modeling/export; rounded nose-cap refinement, original tire stencils, panel seams/fasteners, clearcoat and original carbon/rubber normal maps. Garage work-zone edges, cabinet/tool details, air reels, sockets, tire storage, fixtures and exterior pit-lane context establish scale. Existing detailed wheel guns replace the plain placeholders. Crew proportions and joint continuity were refined, and gun operators' hands follow the gun's actual grip points. Giant overhead-beam shadows were removed. Two wall monitors display actual simulation snapshots on one reused texture. No fake live feeds or unrelated decorative animation were added.

## Remaining work and release limits

The interaction and engineering systems are functional; **the requested photorealistic Ferrari-grade art acceptance target remains unmet**. The original bodywork is not an exact SF-26 surface match. Close-up wing/suspension surfaces and livery remain simplified. The crew has visible procedural silhouettes and authored poses, without a skinned human asset, constrained IK, collision/contact solver or motion capture. Lighting uses a generated reflection room and a soft contact approximation, not path tracing or full dynamic room reflections. A further dedicated vehicle/character art pass is needed before calling this exceptional automotive visualization.

No physical iPhone/Android, Safari, Firefox, long GPU soak or public CDN/network deployment was verified. The ML is an educational historical dry-race model, with explicit rival/traffic/neutralisation assumptions; it does not provide a calibrated chance of winning a real race. Naming/likeness rights still require publication review. Unrelated Rally House work in the shared checkout was preserved. No external publication, commit, PR or deployment was created.
