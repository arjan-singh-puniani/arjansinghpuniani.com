# Wheel transform correction · 27 October 2026

The runtime model's tire geometry is centered on its wheel pivots. The wobble came from transform composition: the old XYZ Euler rotation mixed wheel roll with the car's yaw, so a rolling wheel's axle tilted out of alignment during departure. Discarded wheels also continued to take their spin angle from the departing car. Staged replacement wheels inherited chassis lift before being brought to the hub, and attached wheel centers did not include the body's temporary pitch during jack operation.

`hero-car.js` now provides one YXZ orientation helper, with the mirrored side's roll sign handled explicitly. World wheel updates keep mounted axles aligned with the car, freeze outgoing spin after detachment, and start replacement rolling from its installed angle. Mounted centers follow the body/hub transform. Spares remain on the floor until lifted into alignment. The alignment scenario uses one bounded correction rather than a repeated angular oscillation; the preserved mechanical view shares that correction. Other cars' attached wheels use the same orientation helper.

Wheel handlers' grips follow the upper tire when standing, and the existing contact-shadow plane rotates with the car. Inspection also exposed exterior barriers crossing the modeled exit; the barrier/fence and distant facade now have a passage around that path. No Blender geometry or asset license changed.

Validation:

- **147 unit tests passed**, including three new wheel-orientation/rolling/correction regressions. Both sides remain aligned over many combinations of roll and yaw; they roll in the same physical direction and preserve their hub pivots.
- **Five browser wheel checks passed** using the actual loaded GLB and scene matrices across **980 mounted-wheel samples**. Maximum hub-center error: **8.88e-16 m**; maximum pivot-center error: **0**; axle dot-product error: **3.33e-16**. Discarded rotation change and staged height error: **0**. These are numerical geometry checks, not measurements of real tire dynamics.
- The existing **26-check garage challenge suite passed**, covering direct removal/fitting/fastening, keyboard operation, safe release, exact replay, best score, mobile layout and bounded graphics allocations.
- Typecheck and targeted ESLint passed. The local production build was rebuilt and the preview restarted.

The wheel browser script exposes scene matrices only in its intercepted test response; no wheel-debug hook ships in the application. Reproduce with `PIT_CHROME_METAL=1 node tools/pit-stop/wheel-qa.mjs` against the running local preview. Use `PIT_STOP_URL` / `PIT_WHEEL_EVIDENCE` to select another server or evidence directory.
