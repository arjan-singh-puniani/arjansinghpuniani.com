# Pit-wall engineering upgrade — 1 November 2026

This release replaces the default garage-only entry with a connected historical pit wall. It is a substantial engineering and interaction upgrade, not evidence of an objective design rating, hiring outcome or operational racing advantage.

## Reproducible historical evidence

The original OpenF1 Bahrain 2024 race bundle (session 9472) remains the pace-learning source. A new 219 KB archive contains Leclerc and Sainz lap 32: 369 + 370 measured car-data records and 366 + 368 location records. `tools/pit-stop/capture-telemetry.py` retrieves only the bounded two-lap windows. It records exact request URLs, original-response hashes and canonical-record hashes. No credentials or external scripts are required at runtime. The capture is a personal educational analysis of an independent source; it is not an official team feed.

The plotted track comes from measured locations, rotated and uniformly scaled for display. Telemetry traces align by normalized trapezoidal speed-integrated distance. The integrated lengths are about 5.354 / 5.349 km; they are not surveyed circuit distances. Each driver starts their own lap at zero. Markers are a retrospective lap-aligned comparison, not simultaneous race position. Brake is binary pedal status, not pressure. DRS follows the source's documented 10/12/14 codes. Gaps over 1.5 seconds are not interpolated as valid speed telemetry.

## Decisions without future observations

At each selected timestamp (LEC start of lap 24, 32, 40 or 48), only completed laps and already available stint starts, flags and weather enter a fresh data prefix. Known active stints keep their observed start even when their first lap is unfinished; future stint endings are clipped. This preserves exclusion of a known preceding pit-in lap without revealing future service timing. The scheduled 57-lap distance bounds the horizon and does not enter fitting.

At lap 32 the final prefix includes 602 completed laps, of which 483 pass quality gates. 369 earlier laps fit ridge regression and 114 later available laps measure error. Final held-out MAE is approximately 0.251 s versus 0.376 s for the same-coverage constant-pace comparator. These are pace-prediction results, not validated strategy-outcome calibration. Forecasting uses only the fitted earlier rows; held-out residuals calibrate scenario noise.

The UI separates the retrospective telemetry lap from the timestamp-gated learning inputs. Future-mutation tests alter later lap outcomes, weather, race flags and compounds and require identical earlier model weights, counts and validation.

## Action comparisons and intervention

All 9 supported compound/window actions receive shared seeded pace-model, service and event draws. The active deterministic pit model supplies 64 service/queue samples to 1,200 futures. An actual gun-delay intervention changes those samples; an unsecured wheel clears the call and disables export. Paired P10/P50/P90 elapsed differences versus staying out are calculated within each shared future, rather than subtracting independent median times.

A 36-cell robustness sweep varies assumed transit loss (5–30 s) and neutralisation chance (0–75%), using 200 shared futures per cell. Selecting a cell applies its assumptions and reruns 1,200 futures. Changes to the fixed evidence/crew/rival/traffic assumptions invalidate the old map. Input changes cancel in-progress comparisons; invalid or empty numeric entries cannot silently become zero. Exported Markdown contains evidence cutoff, assumptions, configuration, validation, ranked actions, paired deltas, feature names, fitted coefficients, training support and a machine-readable result; a current sweep is included when available.

## Runtime and presentation

The default pit wall uses native SVG and local historical JSON. Three.js, the GLB, the garage and GPU context are deferred until a visitor selects a 3D experience. A needless Three.js import used only to clamp audio pan was replaced by built-in arithmetic. The pit wall does not keep the hidden 3D renderer running. Readouts avoid unchanged DOM writes, and replay pauses at the recorded finish. Reduced-motion users start paused. The original garage, challenge, mechanics and full strategy laboratory remain available.

## Limits

One race, dry conditions, assumed rival gap/stop plan and transit/neutralisation/traffic inputs. No overtaking, retirements, tire inventory/sporting-eligibility solver, full-grid strategy calibration, live operational feed, proprietary telemetry or hiring evidence. Tire-age extrapolation remains visible; no end-to-end real-world win calibration or independent motorsport engineer review has been established. Physical-phone GPU, thermal and assistive-technology testing remain unverified. The original car is an estimated visual study and crew motion is procedural.
