# Scientific claim audit

Audit date: 2026-11-18. Status reflects the exact claim wording and its use in the project. This is an editorial source check, not independent expert review. The JAMA study was checked against its accessible primary abstract; full-text methods and limitations were not accessible.

Primary study: Scott GD, Lim MM, Elliott JE, Neilson LE. “Agent Orange Exposure and Diagnosis of Parkinson Disease and Related Disorders Among US Veterans.” *JAMA Neurology*, published online November 16, 2026. [DOI: 10.1001/jamaneurol.2026.4219](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamaneurol.2026.4219) · [Article abstract](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamaneurology/article-abstract/2856723).

| Claim ID | Status | Claim and evidence | Qualification or disposition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| STUDY-01 | VERIFIED | The cohort included 4,468,509 US veterans who served during the Vietnam War era. | The abstract describes a VA electronic-health-record cohort; exposure was self-reported. It reports 4,917,272 screened, mean follow-up of 12.5 years, and a cohort that was 98% male. |
| STUDY-04 | QUALIFIED | The abstract says cumulative-incidence analyses accounted for competing risk of death, age, traumatic brain injury, and frailty, and lists baseline characteristics balanced across exposure groups. | The exact wording attributes these details to the abstract. Full-text methods, model diagnostics, and limitations remain unchecked. |
| STUDY-02 | QUALIFIED | Self-reported Agent Orange exposure was associated with Parkinson disease diagnosis (adjusted HR 1.44, 95% CI 1.37–1.52). | Association in an observational cohort; not an absolute probability and not proof of causation. The study does not isolate 2,4-D. |
| STUDY-03 | QUALIFIED | The direct or occupational exposure subgroup had an adjusted Parkinson disease HR of 1.99 (95% CI 1.71–2.32). | Subgroup association, not an absolute probability or proof of causation. The abstract defines this group by Operation Ranch Hand, Army Chemical Corps, and/or direct handling or spraying. |
| CHEM-01 | VERIFIED | Agent Orange was a formulation of 2,4-D and 2,4,5-T; TCDD was a contaminant associated with 2,4,5-T manufacture. | The project labels the compounds separately and does not describe TCDD as a herbicide ingredient. Sources: [US EPA](https://www.epa.gov/ingredients-used-pesticide-products/24-d), [PubChem 2,4-D](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/1486), [PubChem 2,4,5-T](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/1480), [PubChem TCDD](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/15625). |
| HIST-01 | VERIFIED | Operation Ranch Hand used modified C-123 aircraft; the opening aircraft is explicitly described as an illustrative C-123 Provider-inspired schematic. | It is not a reconstruction of a specific aircraft, mission, location, or flight path. Source: [National Museum of the US Air Force](https://www.nationalmuseum.af.mil/Visit/Museum-Exhibits/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/195955/down-in-the-weeds-ranch-hand/). |
| ANAT-01 | QUALIFIED | Interactive putamen, caudate, and substantia nigra pars compacta surfaces derive from the CIT168 1 mm MNI template label map; the dopamine pathway is schematic. | The surfaces are group-template geometry smoothed for display, not individual histology or exposure-specific pathology. Source: [Pauli et al., *Scientific Data* (2018)](https://doi.org/10.1038/sdata.2018.63). |
| ANAT-02 | QUALIFIED | The interactive whole-brain reference uses the Allen Human Reference Atlas 3D model, mirrored to form a whole brain and simplified for browser display. | Generic reference anatomy, resized for the Visible Human body, not a veteran scan or exposure finding. The 283 named meshes were display-simplified; no cross-atlas registration to CIT168 is claimed. Source: [NIH 3D HRA model](https://3d.nih.gov/entries/20960?version=1), CC BY 4.0. |
| STUDY-05 | QUALIFIED | Self-reported Agent Orange exposure was associated with REM sleep behavior disorder (HR 1.78, 95% CI 1.68–1.89). | Separate outcome from Parkinson disease; abstract-level verification; not an absolute probability or proof of causation. |
| STUDY-06 | QUALIFIED | Self-reported Agent Orange exposure was associated with dementia with Lewy bodies (HR 1.66, 95% CI 1.32–2.09). | Separate outcome from Parkinson disease; abstract-level verification; not an absolute probability or proof of causation. |
| STUDY-07 | QUALIFIED | The direct or occupational exposure subgroup had an RBD HR of 2.25 (95% CI 1.87–2.70). | Separate outcome from Parkinson disease; subgroup estimate; abstract-level verification; not an absolute probability or proof of causation. |
| STUDY-08 | QUALIFIED | The abstract reports results robust to additional adjustment and multiple imputation; its sensitivity analyses indicated that substantial unmeasured confounding would be required to explain the observed associations. | Author-reported sensitivity analysis, not proof that residual confounding is absent. Detailed methods and sensitivity-analysis assumptions remain unchecked. |
| SCREENSHOT-01 | UNVERIFIED | “44 million pounds of 2,4-D are applied annually in the United States and this is four times paraquat use.” | Source year, use categories, and underlying dataset were not established. This screenshot claim is excluded from public text and narration. |

No claim in this registry is marked INCORRECT. Claims with incomplete evidence are qualified or excluded rather than presented as established facts.
