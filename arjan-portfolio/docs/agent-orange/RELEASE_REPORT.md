## Render and review record

- `public/agent-orange/renders/film-preview/` contains eight rendered representative keyframes from the Blender scene. The opening uses a C-123 Provider-inspired schematic and illustrative spray. Chemical identities are separate; CIT168 surfaces are labeled as a group template; candidate mechanisms are labeled as hypotheses; the final chart uses adjusted hazard-ratio points and confidence intervals with an HR 1.0 reference.
- `artifacts/agent-orange/encoded-contact-sheet-1080p.png` records the corrected 2,520-frame encoded cut. The cell interval was rerendered with its real camera animation after removing an erroneous static-frame fallback; a single valid endpoint pose replaces a black frame at the shot boundary. A full black-frame scan and complete video decode passed. The evidence chart intentionally holds. The aircraft, neuron, mitochondrion, and pathway remain schematic and visibly short of studio-finished cinema; see `CREATIVE_REVIEW.md`.

## Release decision

Do not describe this work as production-ready or deploy it. Remaining release blockers include recording narration, producing a sound mix, synchronizing captions, rendering an appropriate production master, diagnosing the failed Vercel check, opening a pull request with an authorized GitHub account, real-device/accessibility checks, measured performance, full route regression, and individual dependency advisory review. No independent scientific or clinical expert review has occurred. The full JAMA article methods and limitations were not verified beyond the primary abstract.
