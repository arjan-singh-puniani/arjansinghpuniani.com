## Render and review record

- `public/agent-orange/renders/film-preview/` contains eight rendered representative keyframes from the Blender scene. The opening uses a C-123 Provider-inspired schematic and illustrative spray. Chemical identities are separate; CIT168 surfaces are labeled as a group template; candidate mechanisms are labeled as hypotheses; the final chart uses adjusted hazard-ratio points and confidence intervals with an HR 1.0 reference.
- `artifacts/agent-orange/encoded-contact-sheet-1080p.png` records the current 2,520-frame encoded cut. The atlas caption is positioned below the anatomy and was checked in the encoded shot. A black interval encountered during frame rendering was replaced with a held, previously rendered cell view; this removes black frames but leaves a ten-second static hold in that shot. The evidence chart also intentionally holds. The aircraft, neuron, mitochondrion, and pathway remain schematic and visibly short of studio-finished cinema; see `CREATIVE_REVIEW.md`.

## Release decision

Do not describe this work as production-ready or deploy it. Remaining release blockers include recording narration, producing a sound mix, synchronizing captions, rendering an appropriate production master, real-device/accessibility checks, measured performance, full route regression, and individual dependency advisory review. No independent scientific or clinical expert review has occurred. The full JAMA article methods and limitations were not verified beyond the primary abstract.
