# Narration draft

Target runtime: approximately 105 seconds at a measured delivery pace of 130–140 words per minute, with pauses. This script is written but not recorded or synchronized to picture.

## 00:00–00:08 · The long shadow

A war ends on a calendar. Exposure can leave a longer question behind.

## 00:08–00:20 · What was sprayed

Agent Orange was a wartime formulation of two herbicides, 2,4-D and 2,4,5-T. TCDD was a contaminant associated with the manufacture of 2,4,5-T. These chemicals are related by history, but they are not interchangeable exposures.

## 00:20–00:35 · The brain

Parkinson disease involves degeneration of dopamine-producing neurons in the substantia nigra and changes in the connected nigrostriatal system. This anatomical model is schematic. It does not show tissue from the veterans in the study.

## 00:35–00:52 · The cell

A neuron carries signals through a cell body, branching dendrites, an axon, and terminals. Cellular detail can help us see what researchers study. It cannot, by itself, identify what caused disease in a person.

## 00:52–01:10 · What remains unknown

Environmental exposures are studied through many kinds of evidence. Laboratory mechanisms can suggest questions. They do not establish that a specific pathway occurred in this cohort. Association, mechanism, and causation require different evidence.

## 01:10–01:25 · A connected system

When dopamine-producing neurons are lost, signaling through the nigrostriatal pathway changes. Parkinson disease affects movement in complex ways. A population study of diagnoses does not directly measure this biology in each participant.

## 01:25–01:45 · The study

In a cohort of more than 4.4 million US veterans, reported Agent Orange exposure was associated with a higher rate of Parkinson disease diagnosis. The adjusted hazard ratio was 1.44. A direct or occupational exposure subgroup estimate was 1.99. These are relative hazard estimates, not personal probabilities. The study does not isolate 2,4-D, and an observational association cannot prove causation. The war ended. The evidence continues to be examined.
