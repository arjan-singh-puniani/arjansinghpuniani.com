Case note and references

This fictional case depicts a displaced midshaft femur fracture. The femoral head and neck connect the shaft to the hip. The paired distal condyles form part of the knee. OpenStax · Bones of the Lower Limb

The treatment concepts are fracture reduction, an implant within the shaft canal, transverse locking above and below the fracture, and final imaging. Implant locking screws are distinct from skin staples used for surface closure. The last beat in this case shows a dressing. AO Surgery Reference · Antegrade nailing, sections 4 and 9–14
