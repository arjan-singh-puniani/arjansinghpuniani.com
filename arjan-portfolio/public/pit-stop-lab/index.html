MODEL ASSUMPTIONS & SOURCES

Reduced-order educational simulation. Exact event evaluation; seeded independent triangular timing variation. Constant approach deceleration after driver response, capped constant exit acceleration. The entry-to-exit path is a local segment, not a full circuit pit lane. Generic mechanical teaching dimensions; the car is an original SF-26 launch-reference visual study. No proprietary team data or live telemetry.

Stationary = complete stop to departure motion. Service = positioning start to final fastening confirmation. Transit = modeled approach and departure. Total = transit + stationary + any queue/transfer. Race loss subtracts a synthetic no-stop reference at 55 m/s over equivalent longitudinal positions. Result cards predict the completed experiment; the clock and inspector show the current timestamp.

All slider defaults are illustrative. 60 km/h is a teaching choice. The FIA 2026 default limit is 80 km/h and can vary by event. Our departure instant is motion onset, distinct from the FIA regulatory release definition.

05 / MODEL AUDIT
Model audit
Training, uncertainty & limitations
Ridge regression learns race-lap trend, compound-specific tire-age slopes, compound offsets and driver offsets. First 80% of race laps train; last 20% validate. No validation outcomes enter the fit. The comparison baseline is each driver's training mean on that compound.
32 bootstrap fits resample complete stints within each driver. Each trial shares the same model draw and race event across actions; held-out residuals are sampled in 3-lap blocks per car. This represents modeled uncertainty; it has not been calibrated against race-winning outcomes.
Start/pit laps, flagged incident laps and their successors, neutralised periods, and laps over 107% of the driver's training-period median are removed. Remaining traffic, fuel, tire condition and driver intent can still confound learned associations. Weather is dry-session context, not a predictive feature. One race cannot establish performance at other circuits or in later seasons.
