Sources & model

Circuit engine: 1–3 qubits with complex state-vector arithmetic. The calculation view carries exact radicals and phases through supported gates, and retains trigonometric expressions for arbitrary rotations. Pulse scrubbing follows the compiled control sequence. Readout uses seeded Gaussian I/Q samples.

Physical model

The hardware layer follows a tunable-transmon architecture: XY drive, virtual Z frame updates, and a flux-controlled CZ through a coupling bus. Timing and geometry are illustrative. The two-level model resolves computational-state evolution; multilevel leakage, decoherence, cable distortion, resonator transients, and thermal transport sit outside that model. Signal propagation is stretched for visibility.
