## Evidence classification and unresolved source needs

- **Established physical relation:** conservative conduction/storage and a stated TEC approximation. Equation correctness is separate from coefficient calibration.
- **External biological result:** belongs to its named authors, preparation, instrument and endpoint. None is a project measurement.
- **Project-specific software result:** comes from the saved parameters, version, seed and numerical solver. It is a simulation.
- **User-reported hardware:** the reported Arduino/Peltier/NeuroSky assembly; exact identity and functional behavior remain unverified.
- **Hypothesis/proposal:** organoid cooling research, additional instrumentation, and future validation milestones.

Needed before expansion: exact hardware records; authorized raw thermal/acquisition data; sensor and electrical calibration; model-specific material/contact measurements; appropriate acquisition requirements; and a separately reviewed biological hypothesis/assay.
