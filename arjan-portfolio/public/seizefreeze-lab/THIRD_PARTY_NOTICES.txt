SeizeFreeze educational laboratory — asset and library notices

Original assets: procedural device, schematic cranial coverings, studio lighting,
machined-surface texture, plots and page design were authored for this experience.
These are explanatory concept assets, not manufacturing drawings or a surgical plan.
The static fallback embeds a render of the attributed reference anatomy below.

Brain, Male v1.3, Human Reference Atlas / HuBMAP.
https://doi.org/10.48539/HBM929.XKCL.339
Reference head skin: HRA united male v1.5, Human Reference Atlas / HuBMAP.
https://doi.org/10.48539/HBM646.RKKC.257

coordinates converted to millimeters and centered, source materials replaced,
explanatory flaps, coverings and contact added. Source structure identities are
retained; atlas mirroring does not represent a particular person's symmetry.
