Recording methods answer different questions

A salvaged consumer EEG board is not an organoid recording system. Its exact capabilities cannot be assigned until the module is identified.

MethodWhat it samplesConfiguration and principal limitations

ECoGExtracellular field potentials at the cortical surfaceRequires a dedicated electrode/reference and acquisition chain; distinct from scalp EEG and from single-cell recording.

RESEARCH SUMMARY / APPROXIMATELY 90 SECONDS

A testable engineering question

Can localized thermal perturbation be delivered, measured and controlled precisely enough to justify investigating its effects on abnormal neuronal-network activity?

01 / Problem

Drug-resistant focal epilepsy

SeizeFreeze proposes sensing-linked focal cortical cooling. Published animal studies motivate the question; their results do not establish this concept’s therapeutic effect.

Yang et al., rat neocortical study (2002) ↗
02 / Contribution record

Architecture, prototype report and software

The project records describe proposed cooling/sensing architecture and team competition recognition. Arjan Singh Puniani reports assembling an Arduino–Peltier–NeuroSky bench prototype. Component identity, historical division of engineering work and prototype performance require further records.

Authorship and historical project record ↗
03 / Demonstrated in software

Reproducible thermal calculations

The original cortical depth model and this separate nonperfused spherical model conserve energy within numerical tolerance. Seeded synthetic recordings, fault scenarios, assumptions and exports can be inspected and reproduced.

04 / Not established

Biological effect and device feasibility

No SeizeFreeze organoid measurements, seizure suppression, calibrated prototype output or chronic implant performance are presented. A model cannot turn a synthetic burst count into efficacy evidence.

05 / Next falsifiable question

Does an inert test article match the thermal model?

Identify the hardware and compare independently measured electrical/thermal behavior with predeclared predictions and uncertainty. A qualified collaborator would separately evaluate biological model relevance, functional readouts, controls and governance.

06 / Interpretation

Engineering and clinical judgment

Thermoelectric heat transfer, delivered thermal exposure and a biological response are separate propositions. Each needs its own evidence. An in-vitro finding would not establish safety or effectiveness in people.
