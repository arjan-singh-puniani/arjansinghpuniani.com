# Competitive differentiation review

Reviewed 2026-10-19 using primary product documentation and research. This is a compact documentation review, not an exhaustive market survey or hands-on usability study. No claim of unprecedented innovation is justified.

| System | Documented overlap | Workflow BODY//ATLAS is testing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [NIH HRA / HuBMAP](https://humanatlas.io/3d-reference-library) | Ontology-linked, expert-reviewed reference organ geometry; spatially indexed biological data. The [HRA knowledge graph](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-025-05183-6) already integrates semantic and spatial resources. | Keep component placement, intended device action, FDA decisions, and explicit uncertainty together at the selected region. Anatomy indexing and linked data are not novel by themselves. |
| [EBRAINS siibra-explorer](https://siibra-explorer.readthedocs.io/en/latest/getstarted/ui/) | Brain reference spaces, parcellations, coordinate lookup, region selection, linked multimodal data and knowledge graph resources. | A device's physical interface versus physiological target, with application-level provenance and regulatory event history. This preview does not match siibra's atlas or coordinate-analysis depth. |
| [FDA Devices@FDA and TPLC](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/device-advice-comprehensive-regulatory-assistance/medical-device-databases) | Official clearance/approval search; TPLC integrates premarket, adverse-event, and recall information by product line. | Inspect a multi-component anatomical relationship and compare different physical interactions at one target. BODY//ATLAS currently has far less safety coverage than TPLC. |
| [Innolitics FDA search](https://fda.innolitics.com/submissions) | Rich regulatory filters, source summaries, predicate/reference information, and extracted text. | Anatomy-first comparisons with auditable distinctions among external placement, implantation, measurement, and action. Regulatory search itself is not a competitive distinction. |
| [Elsevier Complete Anatomy](https://www.elsevier.com/products/complete-anatomy) | Interactive spatial anatomy, dissection, neuroanatomy and educational material. | Link spatial context to original regulatory and study evidence. This preview has much less anatomical breadth and teaching capability. |
| [Dimensions](https://help.dimensions.ai/en/articles/11454529) | Linked grants, publications, trials, patents, datasets and research analytics. | Device-component anatomy and regulatory semantics maintained within a focused source-inspection workflow. Linking research outputs and funding is already established elsewhere. |
| [Medical-device regulatory knowledge graph research](https://ebiquity.umbc.edu/_file_directory_/papers/1476.pdf) | OWL/RDF representations of FDA regulatory knowledge and query/reasoning workflows. | Make the anatomical assertion itself inspectable and reversible; distinguish physical interface from treatment target rather than relying on automatic compliance conclusions. A biomedical graph alone is not novel. |

The plausible differentiation is integration quality in a narrow task: select thalamus, compare original Activa and ExAblate interactions, inspect source-supported placement/target assertions, distinguish the event dates from retrieval, and export the relevant evidence. The implementation demonstrates that workflow; it has not demonstrated faster research, better discovery, or superiority to competitors in a controlled user study.

Next evaluation: recruit biomedical researchers for timed comparisons against their normal multi-tool workflow; measure source verification errors, task completion, time to a cited brief, and interpretation of uncertainty. Expand only after this produces evidence of useful improvement.
