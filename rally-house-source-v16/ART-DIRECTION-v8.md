# Rally House v8 — Boutique Diorama Art Direction

## Visual thesis

Rally House is a smooth contemporary 3D miniature, not pixel art. The target is a premium animated dollhouse: warm, tactile, rounded, readable at an isometric three-quarter camera, and dense with small domestic details without becoming cluttered.

The user-supplied cozy-game screenshots were treated as reference vocabulary only. No assets or layouts were copied. The useful principles were: miniature room composition, clustered props, plants, warm wood, soft furniture, cute readable characters, and spaces that look pleasant even when nothing dramatic is happening.

## What v8 changes

### Character appeal

- Core adult women now have distinct silhouette metadata rather than sharing the same hair/outfit treatment.
- Mika: ponytail, headband, tennis skirt, warm peach identity.
- Coach June: composed bun, skort, restrained coach silhouette.
- Nia: bob, softer host outfit, social-club silhouette.
- Added a subtly tapered skinned torso variant for feminine-presenting adult characters.
- Faces use slightly larger expressive eyes, tiny catchlights, skin-relative nose/blush tones, layered hair, softer mouth colors, and restrained lashes.
- Added neck continuity, outfit silhouettes, shoe colors, and material categories so characters read as designed people rather than assembled primitives.

### Bonsai identity

Bonsai is now a repeating Rally House signature rather than one generic plant.

Five authored procedural bonsai placements use different silhouettes:

- windswept statement bonsai in the club corner;
- compact pine at reception;
- cascading bonsai by the matcha shelf;
- small maple in the lounge;
- flowering miniature in the pro shop.

Each uses ceramic pots, visible trunks and branch directions, layered foliage pads, and restrained leaf motion. The shapes are deliberately asymmetric so they read as miniature cultivated trees instead of green spheres on sticks.

### Room composition

- Added floor islands/runners to break large bare perimeter zones without touching the tennis court.
- Added reception shoe/bag cubbies.
- Added a court-side racket/towel vignette.
- Increased visible peach, dusty-blue, sage, terracotta, wood, and cream accents at normal camera distance.
- Kept court space visually clean.

### Materials

Renderer now supports a small stylized material taxonomy:

- matte
- wood
- fabric
- ceramic
- metal
- glass
- court
- skin
- hair
- leaf

The shader varies roughness, specular response, and soft grazing response by material. The effect is intentionally restrained: ceramic catches more light than fabric, hair has controlled sheen, skin stays soft, and the court remains matte.

### Lighting

- Preserved v7 directional shadows and cool-sky / warm-bounce lighting.
- Increased practical lamp contribution at dusk/night so evening can read as a lit interior rather than a darkened daytime scene.
- Retained soft contact grounding and reduced the old fake blob shadows so they act more like subtle ambient-contact patches.

## Non-goals

v8 does not pretend to be a professional artist-made GLTF character set. The bodies are still generated by Rally House and driven by the existing 15-bone hybrid animation system. This pass improves silhouette, material response, face readability, hair, outfit vocabulary, room composition and bonsai identity while preserving the simulation.

## Quality bar

Random screenshots should trend toward:

- smooth, not pixelated;
- rounded, not blocky;
- warm, not sterile;
- detailed, not cluttered;
- cute adult characters, not childlike caricatures;
- boutique tennis club, not generic tycoon game;
- bonsai as visual punctuation, not plant spam.
