# Rally House — Color & Character Charm v9

## Goal
Turn the boutique-diorama foundation into a brighter, more readable and more character-forward cozy life sim without adding unrelated systems.

## Color energy
The centralized palette now uses cleaner cream, fresher sage/mint, warmer honey wood, peach/coral, butter yellow and clearer aqua. World architecture was lifted out of the grey-beige midtone band. The shader uses a modest exposure and saturation lift rather than neon color.

## Lighting
Clear-day ambient and direct sun were lifted, sky fill is cleaner, floor bounce is warmer, and contact shadows remain. Rain remains cooler, while practical lights still provide warmth.

## Character appeal
Heads, eyes, hands and shoes are slightly more readable at the gameplay camera. June, Mika and Nia retain adult proportions while gaining clearer face/hair/outfit silhouettes, brighter club colors, distinct iris colors, collars, socks and small club badges. Hair remains layered and keeps secondary motion.

## Locomotion
The walk now has explicit contact/down/passing/up poses and a cadence that better matches travel speed. Characters have separate cadence, bounce, arm swing, hip motion, torso counter-rotation, stride, head stabilization and turn response. Root travel accelerates instead of jumping immediately to full speed. Existing world-space foot locks still win after animation blending.

## Character motion signatures
- June: economical, composed, low bounce.
- Mika: springier, quicker, eager.
- Nia: relaxed and graceful.
- Leo: broader stride and arm swing.

## Scope
No new economy, rooms, currencies or management trees were added. This is intentionally a beauty + motion + charm pass.
