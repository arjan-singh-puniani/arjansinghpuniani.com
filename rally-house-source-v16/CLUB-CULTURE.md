# Emerging club culture — v15

The club tracks training, social, calm, competition and care tendencies. These are descriptive, bounded values, not currencies, unlock trees or needs. Completed practices/advice and lessons reinforce training; actual matches reinforce competition; completed social breaks, reflection and plant care reinforce their corresponding tendencies. Relevant player placements also contribute a small amount.

Culture gently biases subsequent autonomous choices and appears as one plain-language description in the Club Book. It never prevents building, imposes a story route or penalizes changing direction. Since the values accumulate toward a ceiling, very long-lived clubs still need tuning for readable differentiation.

Coaching philosophy is inferred from completed cue use, not a menu choice. The existing evidence system still owns contact/timing/recovery gains; the new philosophy counts and modest confidence effects cannot manufacture technical progress. The Club Book describes the most practiced emphasis.

This release adds no new trophy or gift system. Existing actual-event mementos and match/breakthrough history remain. Multi-day consequences come from retained major memories, pending follow-ups, existing intentions and favorite destinations, rather than a predetermined campaign.
