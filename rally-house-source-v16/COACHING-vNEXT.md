# Coaching — v12

The panel summarizes the last eight observed non-serve Mika contacts. No observations are invented when the player opens it.

Evidence comes from actual released shots in `RallySystem`: preparation is the normalized available contact-animation window; spacing is the correction distance between incoming ball capture and reachable contact target; recovery is lateral distance from the neutral position; cleanliness is the simulated shot outcome. These are game proxies, not motion-capture or scientific accuracy claims. The UI calls this a short preparation window rather than diagnosing a real athlete.

Choosing a drill reserves June, Mika, and court, or queues a request. It does not change coins, development, XP, relationships, or memories. They arrive, June demonstrates using the retained authored/hybrid motion, and practice begins.

Five menu choices map to four cues: early preparation (drop-feed/shadow), spacing, recovery, and rhythm. Preparation temporarily extends the actual stroke window; recovery increases court movement speed; spacing changes receiver positioning; rhythm slows the incoming feed. Contacts feed the evidence buffer. The eighth meaningful student contact closes practice.

Resolution requires at least four clean repetitions and usable cue quality to award development. Gains are bounded to 1–5; a poor block awards no development or coins. XP and the completed coaching memory are awarded only at resolution. Before/after evidence influences gain. Preparation/recovery improvements persist as small motion parameters; general development continues to alter the inherited stroke style and tennis skill.

A breakthrough additionally requires at least six baseline observations, repeated prior short preparation windows, six clean practice contacts, at most two short windows during practice, and meaningful accumulated development. It happens once and creates an important memory and a physical lesson-ball memento. This is a coarse game model that needs human tuning, not a validated coaching curriculum.

An interrupted block earns no completion rewards. Reload safely resets it. Progress, completed memories, and persistent motion parameters survive.
