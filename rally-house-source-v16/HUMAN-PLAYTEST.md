# Independent attachment playtest

Status: not performed. User can arrange an independent tester. Do not give this whole document to the tester; give only TESTER-HANDOFF.md before play.

Use a fresh club, 20–30 minutes, no personality explanation and no tour of hidden state. The moderator records device/browser and exact answers without correcting names or suggesting stories. Do not equate automated activity counts with attachment.

After play, ask in order:
1. Name the four people who were already in the club.
2. Describe each in one sentence.
3. Who surprised you? What happened?
4. Who did something you expected?
5. What relationship did you notice?
6. Did anyone remember something? What made you think so?
7. Did anything you built matter?
8. What do you want to happen tomorrow?
9. Whose absence would you notice?
10. What happened during your visit? Tell it as you remember it.

Then run a UI-light five-minute observation. Ask who seems confident, tired, competitive, or close to someone else. Save the development telemetry separately; compare perception to state only after answers are recorded.

Record exact quotations in HUMAN-RESPONSES.md. Include missed captions, confusing gestures, repeated lines, and whether answers depended on the Club Book. Mark “not noticed” without prompting. Repeat with the same save on another day. Aim for most testers distinguishing all four; spontaneous predictions and specific story recall matter more than a score.
