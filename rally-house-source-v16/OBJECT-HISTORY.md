# Building becomes a place people use — v15

Meaningful placed objects keep stable IDs, placed day, usage count, per-person visits, favorites, comfort and memory/event associations. A revision identifies a move. Decoration does not receive fabricated social history.

A completed positive visit can strengthen preference. Repeated use can establish a favorite; the next composed break or rehearsal can select that actual object. Benches support solo/shared rests, plants support Nia's care ritual, and baskets support Mika/Leo rehearsal. Lamps keep their visual role and can contribute to club atmosphere; they are not seats or practice targets.

Moving a favorite preserves its identity and history, increments its revision and makes one gentle rediscovery available to each affected owner. A completed rediscovery consumes that pending event. Removing an object clears its associated preference and pending destination safely. Neither operation imposes a relationship penalty.

Placement validates the whole relevant footprint, legal floor and nearby characters. Reserved objects cannot be moved out from under their users. Updating bench obstacles refreshes active walking paths. The doorway and lobby access positions were corrected after a route sweep found unreachable schedule transitions.

Tests cover positive visits, different object preferences, missing/removal handling, move identity and rejected occupied placement. Browser QA completes three actual visits before checking a favorite-bench conversation. The long simulation includes player-built bench, plant and basket; reported favorite adoption is observed output, not a seeded favorite.
