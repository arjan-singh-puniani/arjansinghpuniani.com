# PLAYTEST — v9

## Five-minute visual check
Run at normal speed. Watch every core character walk at least twice. Do not interact for the first two minutes.

Record: (1) any foot skating, (2) any head bob that feels comical, (3) whether June/Mika/Nia are distinguishable before reading labels, (4) whether the academy still feels washed out, (5) whether any accent now feels too saturated.

## Freeze-frame check
Pause on random walking, idle and court-ready frames. Reject poses that look off-balance, stiff, childlike or anatomically impossible.

## Daylight check
Inspect morning, midday, golden afternoon, rain and evening. Clear daytime should feel fresh and bright; rain should feel cool outside and warm inside rather than uniformly grey.

## Character question
After 15–20 minutes ask: “Who felt most distinctive, and why?” If the answer is only clothing color, movement/acting still needs work.

## Target-device check
Verify on the actual Mac and a recent iPhone. Use ?debug=1 for frame/render/simulation metrics.
