# Rally House game-feel research

Research informed hypotheses; measurements and the running game selected changes. Commercial references supply public principles, not assets, branding, court layouts or animation clips. No third-party game asset was imported.

Player anecdotes about TopSpin included both enjoyment and complaints about watching timing meters: [discussion one](https://www.reddit.com/r/TopSpin2K/comments/1uk5v0f/this_game_is_not_good_and_te4_is_vastly_superior/), [discussion two](https://www.reddit.com/r/TopSpin2K/comments/1ccxp7w/). These are subjective, unrepresentative observations. They motivated asking whether attention stays on the ball; they do not establish technical facts or substitute for Rally House playtesting.

## Synthesis and stopping point

The strongest actionable mismatch was between an existing generous swing buffer and a prompt that appeared only after the bounce. Predictive information could make that buffer usable without changing legal contact. A small landing cue can clarify destination while leaving movement to the player. A stable court view matters more than dramatic tracking, especially in portrait. Contact feedback already had a coordinated owner and cached material sound; rewriting it without comparative listening evidence carried more risk than fixing anticipation.

For the club, the useful unit is a completed embodied action with coherent consequences. Real surface anchors and saved receipts make existing content denser without expanding the object catalog. Match memory makes a completed competitive event part of the same place. Research stopped changing these hypotheses; implementation and comparative captures became more informative. Subjective pleasure, sound quality and authored body deformation remain the human/asset gates in the playtest report.
