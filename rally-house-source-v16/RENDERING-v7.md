# Rendering v7: shadows, light grading, framing

## What was missing

The v6 renderer had no shadow pass of any kind. Lighting was wrapped Lambert plus a
hemisphere ambient term plus four point lights. Nothing occluded anything. The club read
as flat shaded props floating on a plane, and the only grounding came from hand-placed
translucent blobs in `World.shadow()`.

The camera also used a fixed vertical field of view and a fixed distance, ignoring aspect
ratio. On a phone in portrait the visible horizontal extent was about 13 world units
against a club roughly 22 units wide, so the opening view was a green rectangle.

## What v7 adds

### Directional shadow map
- 2048 x 2048 `DEPTH_COMPONENT24` texture, sampled as `sampler2DShadow` so hardware
  bilinear comparison runs underneath the 3x3 tap loop.
- Frustum is fitted once to a fixed sphere (`SHADOW_CENTRE`, `SHADOW_RADIUS = 17`) rather
  than to the view. Texel density never changes, so shadows do not swim as the sun moves.
- The light matrix snaps its centre to whole shadow texels, which removes edge crawl.
- Bias is normal-offset and slope-scaled, plus `polygonOffset` in the depth pass.
- Shadows fade out over the last 14% of the map so the plinth has no hard cut line.

### Caster filtering
`Renderer.casts()` excludes unlit meshes, anything below 0.92 alpha, and thin meshes
sitting on the floor (`scale.y <= 0.09 && position.y < 0.55`). Without this the court
lines shadow-acne the court. Meshes can also opt out explicitly with `noShadow`.

### Light grading
`World.lighting()` now returns `sunColor`, `skyColor`, `bounceColor` and `shadowStrength`.
- Sun runs cool-white at midday and slides to amber through the golden hour.
- Sky fill is cool, floor bounce is warm, so shaded faces read blue-grey against warm
  sun-side faces instead of everything going the same grey.
- `shadowStrength` drops under cloud, drops further in rain, and scales with daylight, so
  dusk loses its cast shadows instead of keeping midday contrast at night.
- Tone mapping gained a small saturation lift, which stops the pastel palette turning grey.

### Shader deduplication
The rigid and skinned fragment shaders were near-identical copies. Both now build from one
`LIGHT_BODY` string, and the vertex shaders are generated by `rigidVS(depthOnly)` /
`skinVS(depthOnly)`. Four programs, one source of truth.

### Camera framing
`CameraController.setAspect()` reframes for the viewport:
- landscape: 26 degree lens, fits a 12.4 unit radius
- portrait: widens toward 40 degrees, fits a 9.3 unit radius
- `fitDistance` drives `minDistance` / `maxDistance`, so zoom limits scale with the frame
- `autoFrame` stays true until the player zooms; `frameAcademy()` restores it, and the
  camera button now refits instead of jumping to a hard-coded distance of 35

## Measured

Captured headless in Chromium 131 with SwiftShader via `tools/shoot.mjs`.
These are software-rasterised draw statistics, not frame rates. No GPU timing was measured.

| scene   | draw calls | triangles | shadow casters |
|---------|-----------|-----------|----------------|
| clear   | 453       | 143128    | 237            |
| rain    | 518       | 150344    | 238            |
| evening | 465       | 149848    | 237            |

Draw calls are unchanged from v6 in the main pass. The shadow pass adds 237 depth-only
draws per frame. Draw call count is the most obvious remaining performance lever: every
mesh is still an individual `drawArrays` with no instancing.

## Known remaining defects

- P1 Empty floor. Roughly 40% of the framed shot is bare floor. This is now the largest
  store-screenshot weakness.
- P1 Evening reads as a darkened day rather than a lamp-lit room. The four point lights are
  too weak and too few to carry the interior after sunset.
- P1 The palette is warm but monochromatic. The specified peach, dusty blue and terracotta
  accents exist but are too small to register at framing distance.
- P2 The legacy translucent blob shadows in `World.shadow()` and `placementMeshes()` now
  overlap real shadows. They are subtle enough to leave, but they should be removed or
  converted into ambient-occlusion contact patches.
- P2 No instancing. 453 draw calls for a scene this small is wasteful.
- P2 Only one shadow-casting light. Interior lamps do not cast.
