# Authored scenes plus composable life — v15

The original 13 authored everyday scene families remain. SceneGrammar adds small performances assembled from an initiator, optional partner, motive, available place/object, relationship, state and a real event. It returns candidates; it does not move characters or grant development.

Examples include a private rehearsal, June's short notebook ritual, Leo checking the strings he already knows, Nia tending the player's plant, a shared break, Mika asking for a smaller starting point, quiet courtside watching, a result settling in, a witnessed-result conversation, a lesson cue rehearsed alone and rediscovery of a moved favorite.

Dialogue is authored and character-specific, not generated by a language model at runtime. Familiarity shortens greetings. Nia's extra-foam line requires the completed prior ritual. A witnessed-result scene requires the recorded witness. A lesson follow-up requires an actual completed lesson. A win-specific reaction requires the winner in the completed match record; a draw does not become a win.

## Selection and performance

Each composed scene carries family/motif metadata and a minimum interval (normally 180 seconds; rest 210). EverydayLife retains template cooldowns. CharacterMind adds decaying family, pair, location and motif penalties over a 300-second window. Daily rituals receive a same-day penalty, not an absolute prohibition. Strong relevant state, relationship or an unfinished meaningful event can outweigh a penalty.

Activities reserve people and places before travel. Travel must finish before dialogue starts. Rain or a nearby salient rally can interrupt a low-priority everyday scene briefly; the scene resumes. Major matches and lessons remain protected. June can briefly notice Mika rehearsing while on a free scheduled walk, then resume her destination.

A courtside watcher holds attention while the real game continues, with bounded lifetime and cancellation safety. Arriving too late is recorded as arriving after the game, not witnessing it. Built-bench conversations choose the seated pose directly, so a new line does not restart sitting. Warm partners sit closer and linger longer.

Only completion changes memory, relationships, favorite visits, possessions and culture. Cancellation cannot consume an unfinished post-court intention or award practice evidence. Reload safely resets partial performances without replaying their consequences.

## Content ceiling

Composition changes setup, partner, location and relevance; it does not supply an unlimited dialogue library. Silent prop interactions remain simple. There are no arguments, gifts or arbitrary multi-character scenes added merely to inflate coverage. The next content pass should be driven by actual players' repeated-session observations.
