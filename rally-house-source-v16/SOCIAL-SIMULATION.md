# Social simulation — v12

The live director replaces v11's early-consequence social execution while retaining its source and old serialized social state for compatibility. Legacy clock beats no longer claim imaginary rally lengths in the running game.

Mika/Leo practice games reserve the court through travel and play. The north/south constructor order now matches their actual court positions. Normal lateral movement cannot revoke ownership. Scores increment only on simulated point-ending events; net/long faults are attributed to the striker, missed receptions to the receiver. Recent game contacts introduce a small bounded fatigue penalty. This is practice scoring, not ATP rules.

At completion, the result includes score, winner/draw, contact count, errors, and longest rally. Relationship familiarity and rivalry change then. The first recorded Mika win is retained as an important semantic memory and physical ball memento. The loser wants another attempt on a subsequent day. A completed coaching block can motivate an earlier chance to test the cue.

After play, Nia may share a café break or use a player-built place with an available member. Relationship warmth and previous object visits affect companion choice; less-visited available companions get a chance too. Repeated object use establishes favorites. Shared history is visible through optional Club Book memories and future-intention dialogue.

The v11 morning/afternoon/evening routines remain. Committed activities temporarily outrank them. Rain encourages indoor breaks without forcibly pulling people out of activities. Opportunities are bounded by cooldowns and opening hours; no real-world deadlines or penalties exist.

Remaining depth: no tournament campaign, full set scoring, arguments/reconciliation system, visiting cast, or broad social planner. The repeated core cast is intentional. Test whether the short practice-game rivalry feels personal before adding content.
