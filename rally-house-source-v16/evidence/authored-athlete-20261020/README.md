# Authored athlete: review candidate

1. **What looked wrong:** Taylor still read as a toy, with separate body parts, simple hair/eyes, clothing intersections, and hands that did not grasp the racket.
2. **Root cause:** The procedural surfaces used a compact bind pose rather than coherent anatomical geometry. The renderer lacked authored UV/material support. Replay assumed that same compact body, and the retarget adapter treated the grip/palm as the end of the forearm.
3. **Smallest complete fix:** Replace Taylor as one clothed, authored CC0 athlete using the existing authoritative pose. Add bounded texture, alpha-mask and expression channels; fit clothing and transfer surface weights with Blender; preserve independent planted feet and the actual wrist-to-grip connection. Other cast members await art review rather than receiving copies of an unapproved face.
4. **Regression:** The complete `npm test` suite passes, including the real GLB, verified loader, actual Character meshes, wrist/palm registration and replay. Native desktop/touch championship entry took 6.140/6.087 seconds and both produced real three-shot rallies. Final phone capture recorded 163 samples, preserved/advanced the club, released every media track, synchronized within 6.6 ms and produced no Axe violations. Source and served copies match across 104 release artifacts.
5. **Actual perceptual result:** `comparison.webp` shows native screenshots from the same phone-recording workflow at approximately 0.6 seconds, before (commit `deff6e71`) and after this change. The result is a coherent adult figure with fitted athletic clothing, textured hair/eyes/skin, planted sneakers and a visible grip. `seated.webp` and `grip.webp` are explicitly isolated diagnostic views rendered by the production Character/Renderer. They are not artwork or mockups.
6. **Colleague-send test:** Closer, **still no**. This establishes one complete hero and a working art pipeline; it does not establish Valve/Rockstar-level art direction, acting or whole-game consistency. The user’s assessment of the new appearance remains pending. The goal remains active.

`live-performance.json` is a bounded ABBA comparison of the real unpaused championship after an actual serve: 240 frame intervals per run, with matching rally/result phase counts. Viewport was 1280×800 at DPR 2; the production renderer used a 2112×1320 canvas. All medians were 16.7 ms; before p95 was 16.7–16.8 ms and after p95 was 16.7 ms. Neither scene was paused or replaced with a diagnostic stage. All GPU/error checks passed. These results describe this Mac and sampled scenes, not every device or animation frame.

Licenses, pinned inputs and runtime/source checksums are documented in `assets/characters/`. No user reference photographs are shipped. The model is a candidate for further art refinement, and no production deployment or merge is implied.
