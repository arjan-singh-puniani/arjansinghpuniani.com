1. **Wrong:** Taylor’s support arm disappeared into the fitted top during ready/shuffle. Closing the racket grip folded the wrist surface, producing an apparent gap between hand and forearm.
2. **Root cause:** a ready grip positioned far to the right and a posterior elbow guide were inherited from the longer-arm avatar. Separately, the grip bake translated palm skin toward an approximate anchor over a .045-unit fade; its longitudinal displacement exceeded that fade and inverted the wrist surface. A fixed grip roll also imposed unnecessary wrist bend.
3. **Smallest fix:** calibrate the authored hero’s ready hand targets and tilt the racket forward, use a forward/outward ready elbow guide, and let the hand roll around the handle to follow the forearm (with a valid-frame fallback for a parallel forearm). In the existing baker, anchor the hand joint at the fitted closed-finger cylinder center and rebuild inverse frames; curl the fingers without translating the palm.
