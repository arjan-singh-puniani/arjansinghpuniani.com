# Tennis kit and face clearance

1. **Wrong:** The loose sports top and long black shorts missed the blue/chartreuse tennis reference. The ready racket obscured the face, and a new grip exposed wrist rotation discontinuities.
2. **Cause:** Generic sportswear was being cropped rather than tailored as a tennis kit. Compact hand goals were retargeted without a relaxed ready elbow pole, and hand orientations were reconstructed statelessly. Skirt skinning alone provided no contact clearance against moving thighs.
3. **Smallest complete fix:** Fit the existing blue top, shorten the protected undershorts and adapt MargaretToigo's CC0 authored skirt with a clean tennis hem, smooth hip weights and a torso-fitted waistband. Place the ready racket beside the face, support its throat, and advance hand orientations once per actor update. Give the skirt bounded thigh clearance in the shared color/shadow vertex shader; recorded/interpolated rigs use the same correction.
4. **Regression:** Full npm test passed; the authored mesh checks were repeated after the final waistband fit. The actual GLB stays below 40,000 triangles. Root-local joint registration, render purity, grip closure, wrist endpoints, independent feet, face clearance and replay flags pass. GPU readback checks actual production shader positions rather than emulating skinning on the CPU. ABBA runs in a real unpaused championship retained 16.7 ms median and p95 at a 2112×1320 canvas on this Mac; the change adds one draw call. A real phone recording captured 162 samples, synchronized within 8.0 ms, released all tracks and returned zero Axe violations. Desktop/touch runs with Taylor reached serving in 6.208/6.077 seconds, produced real three-shot rallies and returned to the club with the court released and all four actors retained. All 105 release artifacts match.
5. **Perceptual result:** Matched production-renderer diagnostic views show the old face-obscuring racket and long black shorts beside the new clear-faced ready stance, fitted blue top and chartreuse skirt. These are real runtime captures at the same camera/lighting, not artwork. Short WebMs show the actual transitions. Ordinary skin/shorts breakthroughs in the ready skirt and waistband were corrected. The pose's measured steady wrist deflection is about 27 degrees; maximum hand-relative transition steps in the regression fell from about 5 cm to 2.7 cm at 120 Hz. `live-phone.webp` additionally shows the actual in-club phone workflow. Separate live-gameplay reports cover playability and timing.
6. **Colleague-send test:** Closer, still **no**. Face/hair art, convincing acting, natural seated cloth folds and consistency across the cast remain below the requested bar. Human art evaluation remains pending; the goal remains active.

Baseline: commit 63ca59a5 with its matching character assets. `qa/hand-transition.mjs` reproduces the comparison using `QA_BASELINE_RUNTIME` and its sibling assets. `qa/skirt-clearance.mjs` compiles the production vertex shaders with transform feedback, reads the GPU output, and checks finite positions, GL errors and clearance in both passes across six states plus interpolated seated replay. This is a capsule proxy contract, not proof of anatomically exact cloth at every point between sampled vertices; natural seated draping still needs authored corrections.
