RALLY HOUSE — COZY TENNIS ACADEMY

Open index.html in any modern browser.

Controls
- Tap/click the floor to move.
- Tap people or furniture to interact.
- Use the plant button to open decoration mode.
- Play/pause and 0.5x/1x/2x speed are on the bottom bar.
- Sound is optional and starts only after you enable it.

Everything is self-contained in index.html. No network connection is required.
